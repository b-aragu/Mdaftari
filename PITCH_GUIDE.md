# 🎬 Mdaftari Pitch Guide

A comprehensive guide for preparing screenshots, screen recordings, and demo materials for investor pitches.

---

## 📸 Screenshots to Capture

### 1. Landing/Home Page - Overview Mode
**Purpose:** Show the full dashboard with all key information visible

**What to capture:**
- Mode selector (Collections | Payments | Overview)
- Balance summary cards (Received, Paid Out, They Owe You, You Owe Them)
- Search bar with filter icon
- Period selector (This Week | This Month | All Time)
- Summary stats bar (Transactions, Total Volume, Outstanding, People)
- People list with transaction counts

**Demo data to have:**
- At least 5 transactions
- 3+ different people
- Mix of positive and negative balances
- At least one partial payment

**Filename:** `screenshot_overview_dashboard.png`

---

### 2. Collections Mode - Partial Payment Scenario
**Purpose:** Show the core value proposition - tracking partial payments

**What to capture:**
- Collections mode active (button highlighted)
- Person with partial payment visible
- Example: "JOHN DOE - Expected: Ksh 50,000, Received: Ksh 30,000, Balance: Ksh 20,000"
- The green "Received" and red "Outstanding" indicators

**Scenario to create:**
1. Add transaction: John Doe sent you Ksh 30,000
2. Edit ledger entry: Set expected amount to Ksh 50,000
3. This creates a partial payment scenario

**Filename:** `screenshot_collections_partial.png`

## 🎥 Screen Recordings to Make

### Recording 1: Complete Collections Flow (60 seconds)
**Title:** "Recording a Partial Payment Collection"

**Flow:**
1. Start on Overview mode (3 sec)
2. Switch to Collections mode (3 sec)
3. Tap "Record Payment" (3 sec)
4. Paste M-Pesa message:
   ```
   OKJ789XYZ Confirmed.
   You have received Ksh30,000.00 from
   JOHN DOE 0712345678
   on 15/1/26 at 2:30 PM
   New M-PESA balance is Ksh45,000.00.
   ```
5. Show parsed details (5 sec)
6. Set expected amount to 50,000 (10 sec)
7. Confirm transaction (3 sec)
8. Show the person now appears with:
   - Received: 30,000
   - Outstanding: 20,000 (15 sec)
9. Click on person to show transaction history (10 sec)
10. End on dashboard showing updated balance (8 sec)

**Narration points:**
- "John owes me fifty thousand shillings"
- "He just sent me thirty thousand"
- "I paste the M-Pesa message..."
- "...and Mdaftari calculates he still owes twenty thousand"

**Filename:** `recording_collections_flow.mp4`

---

### Recording 2: M-Pesa Statement Import (45 seconds)
**Title:** "Importing Your M-Pesa History"

**Flow:**
1. Start on home page (3 sec)
2. Navigate to Settings or Import (5 sec)
3. Tap "Record Payment" (3 sec)
4. Paste multiple M-Pesa messages or statement (10 sec)
5. Show transactions being parsed one by one (10 sec)
6. Confirm all (5 sec)
7. Return to home, show all people and transactions now visible (9 sec)

**Filename:** `recording_mpesa_import.mp4`

---

### Recording 3: Mode Switching Demo (30 seconds)
**Title:** "Collections, Payments, and Overview Modes"

**Flow:**
1. Start on Collections mode - show what's owed TO you (8 sec)
2. Switch to Payments mode - show what you OWE (8 sec)
3. Switch to Overview mode - show everything (8 sec)
4. Point out Net Position indicator (6 sec)

**Narration points:**
- "In Collections mode, I see who owes ME money"
- "In Payments mode, I see who I OWE money"
- "Overview shows my complete financial picture"

**Filename:** `recording_mode_switching.mp4`

---

### Recording 4: John's Story (90 seconds)
**Title:** "The John & David Scenario"

**Narrative demo showing the complete problem-solution:**

**Part 1: The Problem (30 sec)**
1. Show a notebook/paper with messy notes (optional, could be graphic)
2. Or narrate: "John is a contractor. David owes him 50,000 shillings."

**Part 2: First Payment (20 sec)**
3. Paste M-Pesa: David sends 30,000
4. Record with expected amount 50,000
5. Show: Received 30K, Outstanding 20K

**Part 3: Second Payment (20 sec)**
6. Paste another M-Pesa: David sends 15,000
7. Confirm transaction
8. Show: Total Received 45K, Outstanding now 5K

**Part 4: Resolution (20 sec)**
9. Show transaction history for David
10. Click on John to see complete payment timeline
11. "No arguments. No forgotten balances."

**Filename:** `recording_john_story.mp4`

---

### Recording 5: Quick Actions Demo (20 seconds)
**Title:** "Navigate Quickly"

**Flow:**
1. Show Quick Actions grid on Dashboard
2. Tap People → show People view
3. Go back, tap Reports → show Reports
4. Go back, tap Settings → show Settings
5. Go back, tap Record → show transaction input

**Filename:** `recording_quick_actions.mp4`

## 📝 Demo Script Template

### Elevator Pitch (30 seconds)
> "Mdaftari solves the biggest pain point in Kenya's informal economy: tracking partial payments. When someone owes you fifty thousand shillings but pays in installments, there's no way to track what's outstanding. M-Pesa moves money, but it doesn't track debt. Mdaftari does. Just paste your M-Pesa messages, and we calculate exactly who owes you what."

### Full Demo Script (3 minutes)
1. **Problem statement** (30 sec) - The notebook/memory problem
2. **Show Collections mode** (45 sec) - Recording a partial payment
3. **Show person detail** (30 sec) - Transaction history and running balance
4. **Show Payments mode** (30 sec) - Tracking what you owe
5. **Show Overview** (15 sec) - Net position
6. **Show import** (15 sec) - How easy it is to get started
7. **Close with value prop** (15 sec) - "Track Every Shilling"
